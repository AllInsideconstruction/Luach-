Recording with the screen locked

Phones switch off the microphone for web apps when the screen locks. To record with the screen locked, use your phone's own recorder (Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder on Android). Afterwards, import the recording here to get the transcript.

iPhone: from Voice Memos
1. Record the meeting in Voice Memos. You can lock the screen. 2. Afterwards, tap the recording, then ⋯ → Save to Files and save it, for example in "On My iPhone". 3. Come back here, tap Import a recording, then Browse and pick it.
Android: from the Recorder app
1. Record the meeting in your phone's Recorder (or Voice Recorder) app. You can lock the screen. 2. Come back here, tap Import a recording and choose it (usually under Recordings or Music, or in the recorder's own folder). If you can't find it, open the recording in the Recorder app and use Share → Save to device first.

Transcripts

Transcripts are made on this phone. Nothing is uploaded. The first time, the phone downloads the speech model once (use Wi‑Fi). After that it works offline. A transcript takes roughly as long as the meeting, or less on newer phones. Keep the app open until it finishes. If it's interrupted, tap Continue and it carries on from where it stopped.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
It opens like a normal app and works without internet.

Recordings are kept in this app on this phone only. Use Save to Notes or Download to keep a copy elsewhere. Remember to tell people you are recording.
